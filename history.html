    Overview

    Southland College was established in Kabankalan City in 2009 with a vision to provide top-quality education in the region. Over more than 15 years it has grown with its students and serves Kabankalan City and nearby communities.

    The founding

    Southland College opened its doors in Kabankalan City with a clear goal: to enlighten enterprising minds and mold the hearts of its students. Honesty, discipline, integrity and respect were set as its core values from the start.
