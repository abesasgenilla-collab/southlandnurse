    Curriculum

    A clear path from the first lesson to graduation.

    How your studies are built

    The curriculum starts with the sciences and basic care, then moves to specialized practice. Each year adds more skills and more time in clinical settings.

    Learning areas

    Core areas shared across the Nursing and Midwifery programs.

    • Health sciences

      Anatomy, physiology, microbiology and other sciences that explain the human body.

    • Nursing and midwifery practice

      Basic and advanced care skills, taught in the classroom and the laboratory.

    • Maternal and child health

      Care for women, newborns and children.

    • Community health

      Health education and prevention for families and communities.

    • Ethics and leadership

      Patient rights, professional conduct and working in a health team.

    • Research

      Learn to read evidence and use it to improve care.
