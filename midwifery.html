    Midwifery

    Skilled, caring hands at the start of life.

    Be there at the beginning of life

    Midwives care for women through pregnancy, childbirth and the weeks that follow, and look after newborn babies. In many communities, a midwife is the first and closest health worker a family knows.

    In the Midwifery program at Southland College, you will learn safe, respectful care for mothers and babies. You will study in class, practice in the laboratory and train alongside experienced professionals.

    What you will learn

    A strong base in health sciences, then practice in the core areas of midwifery.

    • Prenatal care

      Monitor the health of mothers and babies during pregnancy and prepare families for birth.

    • Labor and delivery

      Learn to support normal birth, recognize danger signs and act safely.

    • Postpartum and newborn care

      Care for mothers after birth and keep newborns healthy in their first weeks.

    • Women's health and family planning

      Give counseling and education that help women and families make informed choices.

    Where this can take you

    Care is needed in every community.

    • Midwife in a birthing facility
    • Rural health midwife
    • Hospital or clinic midwife
    • Community maternal health worker
    • Maternal health program coordinator
    • Midwifery educator

    To our incoming freshmen

    Choosing a course is a big decision, and it is normal to feel unsure. You do not need to know everything on your first day. You only need compassion, the willingness to work hard and the courage to ask questions.

    Our faculty will guide you from your first year to graduation. You will learn with classmates who become lifelong friends, and you will take part in training that prepares you for real work.

    Every new life deserves a skilled and caring hand. Let it be yours.

    We would be proud to see you grow as a midwife here. Welcome to Southland College.
