    Nursing

    Care with skill, compassion and courage.

    Care for people at their most important moments

    Nurses are with patients when they are sick, recovering and learning to stay well. They observe, act quickly and comfort, and they are among the most trusted people in any health team.

    In the Nursing program at Southland College, you will learn the science and the practice of patient care. Lessons in the classroom are matched with skills practice and clinical training, with teachers who support you every step.

    What you will learn

    A strong base in science, then practice in the core areas of nursing.

    • Fundamentals of nursing

      Learn basic care, patient safety and how to communicate with patients and their families.

    • Adult health nursing

      Care for adults with common illnesses, from assessment to recovery.

    • Maternal and child health

      Support mothers, babies and children through every stage of growth.

    • Community health nursing

      Bring health education and care to homes, schools and barangays.

    To our incoming freshmen

    Choosing a course is a big decision, and it is normal to feel unsure. You do not need to know everything on your first day. You only need compassion, the willingness to work hard and the courage to ask questions.

    Our faculty will guide you from your first year to graduation. You will learn with classmates who become lifelong friends, and you will take part in training that prepares you for real work.

    Every great nurse began as a student who chose to care.

    We would be proud to see you begin your nursing career here. Welcome to Southland College.
